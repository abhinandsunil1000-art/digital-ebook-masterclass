# Chapter 4: Building Your Audience

## Community Development

### Email List
- Start building before launch
- Offer value in exchange for emails
- Segment your audience
- Send regular valuable updates

### Social Media
- Share your knowledge
- Engage authentically
- Build relationships
- Use platforms strategically

### Content Marketing
- Blog posts
- Videos
- Podcasts
- Guest appearances

## Engagement Strategies

1. **Provide value first** - Build trust through free content
2. **Be consistent** - Show up regularly
3. **Listen actively** - Respond to feedback
4. **Create community** - Foster connections

## Launch Preparation

### Pre-Launch Activities
- Build email list (aim for 100+ subscribers)
- Create social media presence
- Develop waitlist
- Get early feedback from beta testers

### Launch Day Strategy
- Announce across all channels
- Engage with early customers
- Gather testimonials
- Share social proof

## Post-Launch Growth

- Maintain regular communication
- Continuously improve based on feedback
- Expand to new channels
- Leverage customer success stories
