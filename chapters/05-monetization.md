# Chapter 5: Monetization Strategies

## Pricing Models

### One-Time Purchase
- Ebooks
- Courses
- Templates
- Software licenses

**Pros:** Simple, predictable
**Cons:** One-time revenue per customer

### Subscription/Membership
- Access to ongoing content
- Community features
- Regular updates

**Pros:** Recurring revenue, predictable income
**Cons:** Requires consistent content delivery

### Freemium Model
- Free version with limited features
- Premium version with advanced features

**Pros:** Large user base, conversion potential
**Cons:** Must support free tier

## Pricing Strategy

### Factors to Consider
- Production costs
- Market rates
- Value provided
- Customer willingness to pay
- Competitive landscape

### Pricing Approaches
- **Cost-plus:** Cover costs + profit margin
- **Value-based:** Price based on value delivered
- **Market-based:** Match competitor pricing
- **Psychological:** Use price anchoring tactics

## Payment Processing

Choose reliable payment processors:
- Stripe
- PayPal
- Gumroad
- Teachable
- Kajabi

## Revenue Optimization

1. **Test different prices** - A/B test pricing
2. **Offer bundles** - Increase average order value
3. **Create tiers** - Appeal to different segments
4. **Upsells & cross-sells** - Expand customer lifetime value
