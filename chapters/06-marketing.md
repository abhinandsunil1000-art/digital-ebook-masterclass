# Chapter 6: Marketing & Distribution

## Distribution Channels

### Direct Sales
- Your own website
- Email list
- Social media

### Marketplaces
- Gumroad
- Teachable
- Amazon KDP (for ebooks)
- AppStore/Google Play

### Affiliate Partnerships
- Partner with influencers
- Commission-based promotion
- Leverage their audience

## Marketing Tactics

### Content Marketing
- Blog posts demonstrating expertise
- Free resources that lead to your product
- Video tutorials
- Podcast interviews

### Paid Advertising
- Facebook/Instagram ads
- Google ads
- Influencer sponsorships
- Native advertising

### Organic Growth
- SEO optimization
- Social media engagement
- Community participation
- Referral programs

## Measuring Success

### Key Metrics
- Conversion rate
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
- Lifetime value (LTV)
- Return on ad spend (ROAS)

### Analytics Tools
- Google Analytics
- Mixpanel
- Amplitude
- Custom dashboards

## Building Momentum

1. Launch with a bang
2. Encourage early reviews/testimonials
3. Share success stories
4. Double down on what works
5. Continuously optimize
